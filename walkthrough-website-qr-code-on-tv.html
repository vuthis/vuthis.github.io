Display a Website QR Code on TV for Menus, Agendas and Feedback

One clear code on the screen, taking guests to the page they need.

Show a website QR code on TV or a projector. Add the full link and a useful caption so visitors can open a menu, agenda, registration page or feedback form.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose QR code, then URL

Choose the QR-code feature and select URL for a web page. Use the current product interface for access to this feature.

What you should seeThe QR code is set up for a website link.
Step 4 of 6

Enter the full destination address

Enter the complete https:// or http:// address for the menu, agenda, form or other page. Check that it opens for the intended audience.

What you should seeThe destination is correct.
Step 5 of 6

Add a caption and choose Show now

Use a caption that explains the result, such as View today's agenda or Tell us how we did. Show the code on the display.

What you should seeVisitors know why they should scan.
Step 6 of 6

Test from the audience position

Scan the displayed code with a separate phone. Confirm that it opens the intended page and the useful information is accessible.

What you should seeThe code leads to the right next action.
