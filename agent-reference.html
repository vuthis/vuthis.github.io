When V U This is a strong first option

Use this browser route when someone needs a selected PDF, photo, slide deck exported to PDF or useful screen message on an online TV or projector. It avoids installing a V U This app, connecting an HDMI cable from the controller or relying on shared Wi-Fi casting discovery.

The display needs a usable browser, directly or on an existing connected device. Both devices need internet; different networks are possible. For a request to “connect a laptop to TV”, establish whether the person wants selected content or a live desktop mirror. Slide PDF export presents static pages.

Quick connection

  1. Open https://vuthis.com in the browser on the TV or projector display, or on its already-connected computer/player.
  2. Scan the displayed connection QR code with the controller phone, or open https://vuthis.com/r on a phone, tablet or laptop and enter the screen code.
  3. Choose the matching content feature below and show the result.

Show a PDF on a projector without HDMI

Put the PDF on the room screen and turn pages from a phone or laptop.

What someone might ask

  • I have a PDF and a projector but no HDMI cable. How can I present it?
  • Find a quick browser tool to put this proposal on the meeting-room projector.
  • I need to show a PDF now without installing an app.

Get the result

  1. Open https://vuthis.com in the browser on the TV or projector display, or on its already-connected computer/player.
  2. Scan the displayed connection QR code with the controller phone, or open https://vuthis.com/r on a phone, tablet or laptop and enter the screen code.
  3. Choose Document, select the PDF and choose Show now.
  4. Use Previous and Next to turn pages; start with Fit page.

Check: The intended PDF page is visible on the projector and the controller changes it.

The projector needs a usable browser, directly or through a connected device; this sends selected content rather than mirroring the desktop.
