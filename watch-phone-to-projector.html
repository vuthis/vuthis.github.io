Get the result

  1. Open vuthis.com in the display’s usable browser or on the computer already driving it.
  2. Scan the screen’s QR code from your phone. A laptop can connect at vuthis.com/r with the display code.
  3. Choose Document, select the PDF and choose Show now. Use Previous and Next to turn pages. Fit page keeps a complete slide visible; Fit width enlarges a tall page.

No app installation or HDMI cable from your controlling device. Both devices need internet access; shared Wi-Fi pairing is not required.
