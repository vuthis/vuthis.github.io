Show Photos and Run a TV Slideshow Without Casting

The photos you choose on the TV. Move through them yourself or let the slideshow run.

Show photos from your phone on TV without casting. Follow photo selection, order, slideshow timing, Pause, Resume, Fit and Fill in an interactive walkthrough.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose Photos and add your pictures

Choose Photos and select the images you want everyone to see. Drag the photo list into the intended order before showing it.

What you should seeOnly the intended photo set is selected.
Step 4 of 6

Choose Show now and check the first photo

Tap Show now. Use Previous and Next to move through individual pictures from the controller.

What you should seeThe selected photo appears on TV.
Step 5 of 6

Set Slideshow, timing and Start over

Turn Slideshow on for automatic advance. Set Seconds per photo and choose Start over when the set should repeat.

What you should seeThe photos advance at the selected pace.
Step 6 of 6

Choose Fit or Fill and control playback

Fit keeps the whole image. Fill covers the screen and can crop edges. Pause or Resume the slideshow from your device when the room needs a closer look.

What you should seeYour slideshow has the intended framing and pace.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • Fit protects faces, captions and picture edges. Check the crop before choosing Fill for a portrait photo.
  • A photo slideshow is for pictures. Use Queue when the screen should alternate photos with notices, PDFs and other features.

Read the screen from the audience position. Keep the selected content clear, current and useful for the people in the room.
