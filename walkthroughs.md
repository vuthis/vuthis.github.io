# V U This interactive TV and projector walkthroughs

Open https://vuthis.com on the display; connect a phone, tablet or laptop using the display code at https://vuthis.com/r.

## Connect a Phone or Laptop to TV Without HDMI: Interactive Walkthrough

Your chosen content on the room screen. The controls stay with you.

1. **Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector** Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen. Result: The screen is ready for a controller.
2. **Connect your phone, tablet or laptop** Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks. Result: The content controls are on your device.
3. **Choose what the room should see** Choose Document for a PDF, Photos for pictures, Text for a short message or another screen feature. The TV displays your selected content. Result: You have selected the right content mode.
4. **Prepare the content on your controller** Select the intended file or enter the message. Check the controller preview before you put it in front of the room. Result: The intended content is ready.
5. **Choose Show now** Tap Show now to put the selected content on the display. Keep your controls on the phone or laptop while the audience sees the result. Result: The room sees the selected content.
6. **Follow the feature walkthrough** Choose the PDF, slides, photos or screen-message guide below for the exact controls. Both devices can use different internet connections. Result: You can change the display from the controller.
