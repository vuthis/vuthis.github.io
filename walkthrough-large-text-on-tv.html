Display Large Text and Welcome Messages on TV

One clear message, readable from across the room.

Put large text on TV or a projector from your phone. Follow Text, message size, nine positions and colours to create a readable welcome or instruction screen.

Download the printable step-by-step guide

All six steps are below. The downloadable PDF is ready to print.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose Text and write the message

Choose Text. Enter the short message the room needs: Welcome, Registration this way, Meeting in room 4 or Back at 2:00.

What you should seeThe message says one clear thing.
Step 4 of 6

Choose S, M, L or Max

Use L for one large line or Max when the message should fill the screen and may wrap. S and M leave more space around the words.

What you should seeThe message has the intended scale.
Step 5 of 6

Set the position and colours

Choose one of nine positions and a readable text/background combination. Keep the words clear from the farthest audience position.

What you should seeThe layout suits the screen and the room.
Step 6 of 6

Choose Show now and update when needed

Show the message on the display. Change the wording, position or available styling from the controller when the next instruction is needed.

What you should seeThe room has the current instruction.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • A short message is easier to read from a distance than a dense paragraph.
  • Use Announcement when you need a separate title, subtitle and footer.

Read the screen from the audience position. Keep the selected content clear, current and useful for the people in the room.
