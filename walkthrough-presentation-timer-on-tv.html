Countdown, Count-Up and Presentation Timer on TV

A clean timer for the room, with timing controls on your device.

Control a full-screen TV or projector timer from your phone. Explore Down, Up and To a time, with labels, Pause, Resume and Restart for meetings and workshops.

All six steps are below. The downloadable PDF is ready to print.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose Timer and a mode

Choose Down for a set duration, Up for elapsed time or To a time for a fixed clock-time target.

What you should seeThe timer matches the activity.
Step 4 of 6

Set the duration or target and label

Enter the duration or finishing time. Add a label such as Break, Questions, Presentation time or Group work. Choose readable colours.

What you should seeThe room knows what the time refers to.
Step 5 of 6

Choose Show now and run the timer

Show the timer on the shared display. The audience sees the large timer while the controller keeps its buttons.

What you should seeThe room can follow the time.
Step 6 of 6

Pause, resume or restart the appropriate mode

Down and Up support Pause, Resume and Restart. To a time stays tied to the fixed target. At the end of a countdown, the timer and background colours swap.

What you should seeThe timer follows the event rather than distracting from it.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • To a time stays tied to its target; it does not pause like a duration timer.
  • Check the displayed value before the audience starts a timed activity.
