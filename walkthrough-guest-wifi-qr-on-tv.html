Show a Guest Wi-Fi QR Code on a Hotel or Meeting-Room TV

Guest-network joining details in one scannable code on the screen.

Display a guest Wi-Fi QR code on TV. Enter authorised network details, add a clear caption and test joining from another phone for meetings, hotels and events.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose QR code, then Wi-Fi

Choose QR code and select Wi-Fi. Use guest-network details you are authorised to show to this audience.

What you should seeThe code is set up for guest-network access.
Step 4 of 6

Enter the network and security details

Enter the network name exactly. Choose the correct security type and add the password when the guest network requires one.

What you should seeThe joining details match the guest network.
Step 5 of 6

Add a caption and show the code

Use a clear caption such as Guest Wi-Fi: scan to join. Choose Show now and check the code is visible where guests stand.

What you should seeGuests can identify the joining code.
Step 6 of 6

Test joining with another phone

Scan from a separate compatible phone. Confirm it offers the correct network and complete any venue sign-in steps that network requires.

What you should seeThe guest joining route has been tested.
