Turn a TV into a Digital Notice Board

A structured announcement that tells everyone what changed and what to do next.

Display announcements on TV from your phone. Build a notice with title, subtitle and footer, preview the hierarchy and update a reception or event screen.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose Announcement and write the title

Choose Announcement. Give the notice a short title such as Session change, Welcome or Today at reception.

What you should seeThe main point is immediately clear.
Step 4 of 6

Add the subtitle and footer

Use the subtitle for the useful detail and the footer for a time, room or next action. For example: Workshop moved to Hall B; Follow signs upstairs.

What you should seeThe notice has a readable information hierarchy.
Step 5 of 6

Choose colours and check the layout

Choose readable colours. If your current product access includes an image, add one when it helps explain the notice. Preview how the words fit.

What you should seeThe important detail stands out.
Step 6 of 6

Show the notice and keep it current

Choose Show now. Update the announcement from the controller when plans change, then clear or replace it when it no longer applies.

What you should seeVisitors see the current announcement.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • Use title, subtitle and footer for different jobs rather than repeating the same words.
  • Keep private visitor information out of public-facing notices.

Read the screen from the audience position. Keep the selected content clear, current and useful for the people in the room.
