Show a Full-Screen Clock or World Time on TV

One large labelled clock for the time the room needs to follow.

Put a full-screen clock on TV and control it from your phone. Choose local time or a time zone, a clear label, 12/24-hour format, seconds and date.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose Clock and the time source

Choose Clock. Select Local time for the display browser time zone, or Time zone for another place.

What you should seeThe clock follows the intended location.
Step 4 of 6

Pick the zone and add a label

For another time zone, choose the place the room needs to follow. Add a label such as New York, London office or Event time.

What you should seeEveryone knows which time they are seeing.
Step 5 of 6

Set 12/24-hour format, seconds and date

Choose the time format. Turn seconds or the date on when they are useful, and select readable colours.

What you should seeThe time detail suits the room.
Step 6 of 6

Choose Show now

Put the clock on the display. Change its settings from the controller while the screen keeps the time visible.

What you should seeThe room has one clear shared clock.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • Local time follows the display browser time zone. Select Time zone when you want a specific place.
  • Use Timer for time remaining or elapsed time; use Clock for the current time.
