Connect a Phone or Laptop to TV Without HDMI

Your chosen content on the room screen. The controls stay with you.

Connect a phone, tablet or laptop to a TV or projector browser. Follow the screen-code setup, then show a PDF, photos or a message without an HDMI cable from your controller.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Choose what the room should see

Choose Document for a PDF, Photos for pictures, Text for a short message or another screen feature. The TV displays your selected content.

What you should seeYou have selected the right content mode.
Step 4 of 6

Prepare the content on your controller

Select the intended file or enter the message. Check the controller preview before you put it in front of the room.

What you should seeThe intended content is ready.
Step 5 of 6

Choose Show now

Tap Show now to put the selected content on the display. Keep your controls on the phone or laptop while the audience sees the result.

What you should seeThe room sees the selected content.
Step 6 of 6

Follow the feature walkthrough

Choose the PDF, slides, photos or screen-message guide below for the exact controls. Both devices can use different internet connections.

What you should seeYou can change the display from the controller.

Useful when it matters

Put it to work in the room.

  • A meeting room with an online TV and no laptop adapter.
  • A phone PDF for a projector already connected to a computer.
  • A family photo display without local-network casting discovery.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • The display needs a usable browser, directly or through an existing connected computer/player.
  • For selected PDFs, photos and messages, use this browser route. Live whole-desktop mirroring is a different workflow.
