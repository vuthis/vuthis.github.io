PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote on TV Without HDMI

Slide pages on the room screen. You decide when the next one appears.

Show PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote or Canva slide pages on TV or a projector. Export to PDF, connect a controller and advance slides without an HDMI cable from your laptop.

All six steps are below. The downloadable PDF is ready to print.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Export your presentation as PDF

In PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote or Canva, export or download the presentation as PDF. Check the exported page order and layout.

What you should seeYour presentation is ready as a PDF.
Step 4 of 6

Choose Document, then Show now

Select the exported slide PDF in Document and choose Show now. The presentation pages appear on the shared screen.

What you should seeThe first slide page is on the display.
Step 5 of 6

Choose Fit page for complete slides

Use Fit page so the entire slide is visible. A landscape presentation usually suits a widescreen TV or projector.

What you should seeThe whole slide is visible.
Step 6 of 6

Advance the slide pages as you present

Use Previous and Next on the controller. Keep notes and other device activity on your laptop or phone while the room sees the selected PDF.

What you should seeThe presentation moves at your pace.

Get the result right

A quick screen check.

  • PDF export shows static slide pages. Animations, transitions and embedded video are not carried through this route.
  • Before the meeting, open the exported PDF to check fonts, image placement and slide order.

Read the screen from the audience position. Keep the selected content clear, current and useful for the people in the room.
