Try the PDF on the room’s screen

  1. Save the handbook on your controlling phone or laptop.
  2. Open vuthis.com in the display browser and connect.
  3. Choose Document, select the PDF and choose Show now.
  4. Use Previous and Next; start with Fit page.

For the demonstration, use the video walkthrough library. The written PDF/projector instructions explain the controls.
