Build a Digital Signage Playlist on TV Without a New Media Player

One screen moving through the content you prepared, in the order the room needs.

Create a browser digital signage playlist with photos, PDFs, text, announcements, clocks, timers, counters and QR codes. Follow Add to queue, order, Auto and Loop.

Step 1 of 6

Open vuthis.com on the TV or projector

Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already connected to it. Keep the connection code on screen.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for a controller.
Step 2 of 6

Connect your phone, tablet or laptop

Scan the screen QR code with your phone. On a laptop or tablet, open vuthis.com/r and enter the screen code. Both devices need internet; they can use different networks.

What you should seeThe content controls are on your device.
Step 3 of 6

Create content and choose Add to queue

Prepare an item in its feature, then choose Add to queue instead of Show now. Repeat for the photos, PDF, notice, clock, timer or QR code the display needs.

What you should seeYour screen content is collected in Queue.
Step 4 of 6

Drag the Queue into the right order

Arrange the items in the sequence the audience should see. Remove outdated items. Use an item's Show now when something needs the screen immediately.

What you should seeThe running order matches your event or venue.
Step 5 of 6

Choose manual play, Auto and Loop

Use Play queue for manual progression. Turn Auto on for automatic advance, set Seconds per item and enable Loop when the sequence should repeat.

What you should seeThe display follows your chosen playback pattern.
Step 6 of 6

Preview the whole sequence

Watch the transitions. Slideshows and countdowns can hold Auto until their own finish; ordinary items use the Queue timing. The display continues until the V U This session ends.

What you should seeThe screen is ready for the audience.
